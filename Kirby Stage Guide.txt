KIRBY'S RETURN TO DREAM LAND - STAGE GUIDE
For Kirby Access. Plain text, written for screen readers.

You don't need to read this guide to play, and it spoils the stages: where
the keys, switches, Energy Spheres and Super Ability rifts are, and how to
beat each boss. It can be helpful if you find yourself getting stuck, or
wondering what you should be looking for. Each stage has its own heading,
so you can read only the part you need.

Facts gathered from WiKirby stage pages, rewritten and condensed. Wii
version (the one in your Kirby Wii Recomp).

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
Each level has its own heading line starting with LEVEL. Each stage starts with STAGE.
Energy Spheres are marked with the word SPHERE so you can search for it.
Bosses are marked with the word BOSS.

=====================================================================
GENERAL TIPS
=====================================================================

Energy Spheres: 120 in total. They are the gears from Magolor's ship. You do not need them
to finish the story, only for 100 percent and for unlocking extras on the Lor Starcutter.
Standing in front of a stage door on the level map tells you how many spheres the stage
has and which ones you already got.

Sphere Doomers: almost every Super Ability (Ultra Sword, Monster Flame, Flare Beam, Snow Bowl,
Grand Hammer) is used to smash one big special object at the end of its section. That opens
a rift to Another Dimension. Inside, a wall chases you, then you fight a Sphere Doomer.
Beating a Sphere Doomer always gives TWO spheres. If you miss a rift, the two spheres are gone
until you replay the stage.

Super Inhale: shake the remote (in the recomp with your pad, this is the trigger).
Needed for big Super Inhale blocks and groups of enemies.

Keys: if you drop a key into a pit, or carry it too long through danger, it can be lost.
Key Dee races: a Key Dee runs off with a key; catch it before it jumps into a pit.

Round-trip doors: small side doors that lead into a challenge room and back out to the same spot.
Many spheres are inside one.

Lor Starcutter unlocks by sphere count:
  7 spheres: Sword Challenge
  15: Ninja Dojo sub-game
  20: Copy Ability Room 1 (Cutter, Leaf, Needle, Fire, Sword)
  25: Whip Challenge
  30: Scope Shot sub-game
  35: Hi-Jump Challenge
  40: Copy Ability Room 2 (Stone, Parasol, Whip, Beam, Spark)
  50: Bomb Challenge
  60: Copy Ability Room 3 (Spear, Bomb, Tornado, Hi-Jump, Water)
  70: Water Challenge
  80: Copy Ability Room 4 (Ninja, Wing, Fighter, Hammer, Ice)
  90: Wing Challenge
  120: Item Challenge

Sphere totals per level:
  Cookie Country 13, Raisin Ruins 16, Onion Ocean 16, White Wafers 19,
  Nutty Noon 20, Egg Engines 21, Dangerous Dinner 15. Total 120.

=====================================================================
LEVEL 1: COOKIE COUNTRY (13 spheres)
=====================================================================

STAGE 1-1 (3 spheres). Super Ability: Ultra Sword. Tutorial stage.
Area 1, grassland: easy straight path. Blade Knight gives Sword, Sir Kibble gives Cutter.
  Cave door at the end.
Area 2, cave: narrow winding tunnel with Hot Head and Cappy. Find the key and carry it to the
  locked gate.
  SPHERE 1: behind the key gate, right on the main path. You cannot really miss it.
  A 1-Up sits behind a Super Inhale block. Climb up to the door out.
Area 3, crumbling hillside: a Super Blade Knight appears from a portal. Inhale it for Ultra
  Sword. Swing it to cut through big chunks of hill. Destroy the large mound at the end; a
  rift opens.
Area 4, rift: the rift wall comes from the left, run right. Dash over gaps. Super Inhale the
  blocks before the door. Choose Fire or Sword essence.
  SPHERE 2 and 3: beat the Sphere Doomer. A Maxim Tomato follows. Leave the rift, goal is up ahead.

STAGE 1-2 (3 spheres). Mid-boss: Gigant Edge.
Area 1: climb the ladder to a long wooden bridge. Leafans give Leaf. Warp Star at the end.
Area 2: wooden structure in the woods. Cutter essence to the right over a pit. Rope platforms;
  cutting the ropes drops them.
Area 3, Crackler section: pick up the Crackler (a fire-blowing gadget). Break the metal blocks
  around a switch. The switch raises a bridge over a bottomless pit. Note: you cannot float
  while carrying the Crackler.
  SPHERE 1: near the end of the Crackler area, blast the metal blocks in the ceiling.
Area 4, inside a tree trunk: Needle essence on the bottom platform. Climb up past Waddle Dees
  and Cappies. Side gaps hold food and a Maxim Tomato. A hidden door leads to a small room with
  an Invincible Candy.
  SPHERE 2: at the top of the trunk, in the leaves to the right.
Area 5: choose Fire or Beam essence below. Gigant Edge ambushes from the right; inhale it for Sword.
  SPHERE 3: after Gigant Edge, cut the rope set into a wall with Sword (or any cutting ability).

STAGE 1-3 (3 spheres). No mid-boss.
Area 1: short path with a Sparky. Break the bomb block at the end to open a way down to a door.
  Ladder down into a snaking cave of Shotzos. Spark essence on the third path across.
  The path splits (Shotzo route or Star Block route, Spark helps) and rejoins at a door
  standing on a thin floor.
  SPHERE 1: do not take the door. Hold down to drop through the thin floor under it.
Area 2, blue crystal cave: moving platform with a Blade Knight, bottomless pits. A bomb block in
  the ceiling hides a 1-Up. A Super Inhale block hides food. Hit the switch past the Hot Head
  to open the door.
  SPHERE 2: a gate blocks it; the key is on the far side of the chasm. Carry it back.
Area 3: narrow passage with Waddle Doo and Beam essence. Shotzos on blocks; break the blocks
  to drop them. Break the bomb block on the stepped area to drop all Shotzos at once.
Area 4: spiked cave. Hit the switch. A Key Dee starts racing in a cave below.
  SPHERE 3: race the Key Dee and catch it before it reaches the pit. The key opens the
  gate to the right.

STAGE 1-4 (4 spheres). Super Ability: Monster Flame. Has a secret HAL Room.
Area 1, grassland: drop down ladders. Falling rock pillars along the path.
  SPHERE 1: a passage through one of the rocky cliffs leads to a small grotto with the sphere.
Area 2, bridge with gaps: Bowbies shoot from above. A moving platform leads to a switch; it
  breaks a ceiling and drops an Invincible Candy. Use it to charge through. Maxim Tomato near
  the end.
Area 3, underground: falling stalactites. Pick up the Prism Shield above (hold it over your
  head). A second shield replaces it later; it also blocks Shotzo shots.
  SPHERE 2: at the end of the shield section, in a wall nook guarded by Puppet Waddle Dees.
  Bomb block near the ladder drops more Waddle Dees.
SECRET HAL ROOM: at the door to the next area, do not enter. Walk all the way right, then
  come back left past the door. Sunken rock spires reveal a hidden door. Inside: field with
  stars and food, Star Blocks spelling HAL, and Hammer, Fighter, Ninja and Wing essences.
  A door on the right returns you to the path.
Area 4: open the big bomb block barrier. A Super Hot Head appears among tree stumps. Inhale it
  for Monster Flame. Burn stumps; burn the huge stump at the end to open a rift. (If you lose
  the ability, a lower path lets you continue.)
Rift: wall from the left. Many Comos. Super Inhale the 1-Up and the blocks around it.
  Choose Leaf or Spark.
  SPHERE 3 and 4: beat the Sphere Doomer. Goal is past a few Leafans.

STAGE 1-5 BOSS: WHISPY WOODS. Prize: the ship's oars.
Phase 1: drops 3 apples that bounce along the floor. Inhale them and spit them back; that is
  your main damage. Blows air puffs in two waves; they rise or drop toward you, move so they
  pass. Comos swing down from the leaves.
Phase 2 (half health, Whispy grows): long inhale that sucks you in; shake (trigger) to escape.
  Drops enemies like Gordo and Waddle Dee from above. Bigger air puffs in waves of 3; stand in
  the gaps. Apples still come; keep spitting them back.

LEVEL 2: RAISIN RUINS (16 spheres)
=====================================================================

STAGE 2-1 (3 spheres). Mid-boss: Bonkers.
Area 1, dunes: Whippy gives Whip; its reach grabs items from small ruins. A side door holds a 1-Up
  guarded by Gordos.
Area 2: Flamer fire (or any fire) lights a fuse that fires a cannon. Needle essence near the end.
  SPHERE 1: light the fuse, get in the cannon, and you are shot to the sphere's room.
Area 3, downhill boulder tunnel: boulders drop as you go.
  SPHERE 2: follow behind the two boulders; they smash the barrier at the end of the tunnel.
Area 4, uphill tunnel: boulders roll at you. Hide in the nooks in the ceiling.
Area 5: Invincible Candy, then pits.
Area 6: grab the Parasol if you want, then fight Bonkers; inhale him for Hammer.
  SPHERE 3: break the metal blocks with Hammer. Kabu tunnel to the goal.

STAGE 2-2 (4 spheres). Super Ability: Flare Beam. Mid-boss: Fire Sphere Doomer.
Area 1, oasis: inhale a Water Galbo for Water and surf. Items in the pools, a big cache at the end.
Area 2, rock tunnels: Rocky gives Stone; slide down tunnels to break blockades.
  SPHERE 1: pound the stake (Stone) to open a side door. Inside, a small bomb block puzzle.
Area 3: deep underwater tunnel past Glunks.
Area 4, second oasis: a side door deep in the pool, under a breakable barrier.
  SPHERE 2: inside, break the right bomb block to open the barrier. Then take the Warp Star.
Area 5, ruins: Moai Blocks spit fire. A Super Waddle Doo gives Flare Beam. Smash the statues;
  one hides the rift.
Rift: water cubes floating in the air, Blippers inside, Bronto Burts outside.
  SPHERE 3 and 4: Fire Sphere Doomer. Exit upward to the goal.

STAGE 2-3 (4 spheres). Mid-boss: King Doo.
Area 1: climb ruin steps with ladders; small side passages hold items.
Area 2: vertical passage with several paths; follow Point Stars up for the better items.
Area 3: steps with Shotzos and Gigatzos shooting. Inhale a Twister for Tornado; gliding avoids shots.
  SPHERE 1: past the shooters, Super Inhale a Star Block to open a small room with a Tornado
  essence. Use Tornado to break the barriers to the sphere. Door upper right.
Area 4: Starman gives Hi-Jump. Jump straight up through block barriers.
  SPHERE 2: jump through the barrier in the middle of the column.
Area 5: carry the Maxim Tomato box into the King Doo fight. King Doo gives Beam.
  SPHERE 3: use Beam on the bomb block barrier on the way up.
Area 6, cannon grid: cannons launch you upward.
  SPHERE 4: at the top, take the cannon to the right. Goal to the right.

STAGE 2-4 (5 spheres). Super Ability: Ultra Sword. Mid-bosses: Water Galboros, Fire Sphere Doomer.
Area 1: key just to the right, guarded by a Hunter Scarfy. Carry it up the moving stairs to the
  gate at the top.
Area 2, sand conveyor belts that dump into pits.
  SPHERE 1: break the floor barrier in the conveyor area to reach a side chamber. Hit the switch
  from a distance and hurry through the gate before it closes.
Area 3, dark passage: carry the candle for light.
  SPHERE 2: in a nook in the ceiling.
Area 4: fire blocks. Beat Water Galboros for Water and douse the fire blocks.
Area 5: rows of firing Gigatzos. Carry the Transparency Crystal (makes you pass through hazards).
  SPHERE 3: with the crystal, enter a side door.
Area 6, outside: get Ultra Sword. Cut the huge ropes holding the stone platforms. The rift is
  behind the last one.
Rift: wall from the right, run left. Knock down the Shotzo stairs.
  SPHERE 4 and 5: Fire Sphere Doomer. Then ladder down to the goal.

STAGE 2-5 BOSS: MR. DOOTER. Prize: right wing.
Phase 1: juggles skulls and bombs, then throws them. Inhale them while they float or as he
  throws (bombs give Bomb). If you clear them all before he throws, he skips straight on.
  Spins around the room and tries to land on you. Three big hops ending in a stomp.
  Pulls 2 snakes from his turban; spit them back at him.
Phase 2 (half health, gets big): high spinning stomps 3 or 5 times. A jump and three punches,
  the last reaches far. More snakes or bats. Throws three bombs in a line that drop after a delay.

LEVEL 3: ONION OCEAN (16 spheres)
=====================================================================

STAGE 3-1 (3 spheres). Super Ability: Snow Bowl. Mid-boss: Fire Sphere Doomer.
Area 1, beach: pools, coconuts, ropes to hang on. Warp Star at the end carries you up the cliffs.
Area 2: work down a high cliff; Parasol helps. Cross the Barracu pool at the bottom.
Area 3: second beach with Twister and big coconuts.
Area 4, wooden platforms over water: you get Spear here.
  SPHERE 1: break the bomb block behind a Gordo. A side room with a small key puzzle.
Area 5: avoid the sand castle's burning totems. Super Chilly on the far side gives Snow Bowl.
  Roll over everything, and smash the giant sandcastle at the end for the rift.
Rift: a switchback path down. Switches lower platforms. Some parts flooded, Gordos patrol.
  SPHERE 2 and 3: Fire Sphere Doomer. Then right and up to the goal.

STAGE 3-2 (4 spheres). Mid-boss: Kibble Blade.
Area 1: ladder into the water, swim the channel, out past two Leafans.
Area 2, wooden bridges: Blippers jump up. Kibble Blade gives Cutter.
  SPHERE 1: right after Kibble Blade, cut the rope platform, then dive under the bridge.
  The sphere is in the left wall.
Area 3, deep reef: Spear here. Deep pools with Walfs.
  SPHERE 2: hit a switch in a nook you cannot reach, using Spear thrown at it. A side chamber
  opens; enemies in bomb block cages guard the sphere.
Area 4: Stomper Boot for the spike pits; a second boot appears halfway. Enemies are stepping stones.
  SPHERE 3: at the end of the boot section, use Bronto Burts as steps to a high platform.
  Break the big block in front of a door; the sphere is in a side room in the next area.
Area 5, all underwater: Barbar eels cannot be killed; swim past.
  SPHERE 4: one Barbar guards a cache sealed by breakable blocks. Sphere inside.

STAGE 3-3 (4 spheres). Super Ability: Monster Flame. Mid-bosses: Moundo, Fire Sphere Doomer.
Area 1, waterfall cave: go down each waterfall. A Hammer essence is hidden here.
Area 2: current pushes you through a chasm; spiked moving platforms. A moving floor reveals a
  door to a 1-Up room.
Area 3: current sweeps you up a vertical channel. At the top, choose a switch. A hint on the way
  up shows which one.
Area 4, dry cave: Moundo gives Stone.
  SPHERE 1: right after Moundo, use Stone to sink down a pool with a strong upward current.
  Sphere at the bottom.
Area 5: three intertwined channels.
  SPHERE 2: hit the switch on the RIGHT at the top to reach the cache with the sphere.
Area 6: wind tunnels that switch back and forth.
Area 7: Monster Flame. Burn the merman statues by hitting the barrels in their mouths. At the end a
  Giant Flotzo pops out of a shipwreck; hit it when it pops out and the rift opens.
Rift: floating water with Gordos and Barracu.
  SPHERE 3 and 4: Fire Sphere Doomer. Goal is under the shipwreck.

STAGE 3-4 (5 spheres). No mid-boss.
Area 1: deep pool, press the switch at the bottom to open the underwater ruin.
Area 2, halls with rotating spike bars.
  SPHERE 1: a small air pocket off the main path has a round-trip door. Inside, the key is in a
  ceiling cache. Carry it to the sphere's gate. Bomb block at the bottom to the next door.
Area 3: many passages and switches that open walls.
  SPHERE 2: a side chamber has two entrances. Left holds an Invincible Candy. The RIGHT one, through
  a half-hidden door, holds the sphere, guarded by many Barracu.
Area 4: huge spiked shells chase you through tunnels.
  SPHERE 3: while the second shell chases you, duck into a nook and let it pass. Follow it;
  it breaks the metal blocks over the sphere at the tunnel's end.
Area 5: Balloon Bomb puzzle. Carry the balloon bombs down and set them off on barricades.
  SPHERE 4: blow up the barricade with the second Balloon Bomb.
Area 6: many Barbar, some big and sudden. Spear from a Pierce in a ceiling chamber.
  SPHERE 5: swim to the top of the Barbar room and pierce the ceiling cache with Spear.

STAGE 3-5 BOSS: FATTY PUFFER. Prize: left wing.
Phase 1 loop: rolls into a ball and tackles; jumps and stomps (leaves stars to inhale); spits
  little puffer fish (inhale and spit back); spits water drops (inhale for Water).
Phase 2 (puffs up): falling stalactites you can spit back, a water cannon stream along the floor,
  bounces around the room inflated, double rolls, more fish and Blippers.

LEVEL 4: WHITE WAFERS (19 spheres)
=====================================================================

STAGE 4-1 (3 spheres). Mid-boss: Gigant Edge.
Area 1: snowfield with igloos. Igloos hold food, enemies, and elemental blocks.
  SPHERE 1: use Fire to melt the ice blocks inside an igloo.
Area 2: steps; a Crackler helps with the enemies.
Area 3: cannons over pits; some shoot you into the abyss, aim with care.
  SPHERE 2: shoot upward into a hidden passage in the ceiling here.
  The path splits near the end. Take the LOWER, less visible way for sphere 3.
Area 4, lower ice tunnel with icicle spikes: Walky gives Mike.
  SPHERE 3: above a spike pit in the lower tunnel.
Area 5: Gigant Edge in a big igloo. Whip essence before the goal reaches a 1-Up.

STAGE 4-2 (4 spheres). Super Ability: Grand Hammer. Mid-bosses: Super Bonkers, Spark Sphere Doomer.
Area 1: fissures spit Mopoo, snowmen crumble on you. Moonja gives Ninja.
  SPHERE 1: Fire lights a fuse behind an ice barricade. Take the cannon into a room, go through
  the gate quickly; spikes on the far side.
Area 2: falling icicles.
Area 3: icy slopes; a Stomper Boot for the spike pits. Rockies at the door.
  SPHERE 2: in a spike pit, break blocks to find a round-trip door. Inside, ride the rising
  Mopoo as steps up to the ledge.
Area 4: slope, Stone slide (do not slide too long).
Area 5: Super Bonkers gives Grand Hammer. Smash the boxes; two extra-large boxes at the end
  open the rift.
Rift: keys and gates, spit Waddle Dees. Crackler for the Puppet Waddle Dees on tall barriers.
  SPHERE 3 and 4: Spark Sphere Doomer. Goal to the right.

STAGE 4-3 (4 spheres). Mid-boss: Water Galboros.
Area 1: pull switch opens the way down. Coldtzo fire. Door is in a pool.
Area 2, underwater, bouncing spiked shells.
  SPHERE 1: carry Spear across, hit the floor switch; the pit deepens and shows the sphere.
Area 3: rolling ice boulders.
  SPHERE 2: carry the Transparency Crystal up, breaking ice blocks. Go through the crystal gate,
  break the bomb block.
Area 4: swim up waterfalls and water columns over a long pit; Barbar near the end.
  SPHERE 3: with Cutter, drop the heavy rope platform holding a round-trip door (do not get
  crushed). Stand on the left platform, charge a big boomerang, bounce it off the wall.
  Inside, release an ice boulder to reach the sphere.
Area 5: big cavern, Balloon Bombs make a path. A Balloon Bomb also hurts Water Galboros.
  SPHERE 4: after Galboros, carry the key up the moving platform to its door. Hit the nearby switch
  with Water to move the platform. Inside, a bomb block barrier guards the sphere.

STAGE 4-4 (4 spheres). Super Ability: Snow Bowl. Mid-boss: Spark Sphere Doomer.
Area 1: Blade Knight guards a barrier; wind and a snow conveyor belt carry you.
Area 2: second conveyor with many Gordos.
  SPHERE 1: grab the cannon; it fires you to an upper room.
Area 3: wide tunnel, Prism Shields; strong updrafts carry you over pits.
Area 4: tight tunnels going down, a Key Dee races in a parallel tunnel.
  SPHERE 2: beat the Key Dee to its key and open the gate.
Area 5: Super Chilly gives Snow Bowl. Knock down rows of spiky ice pins (all pins in a row = a
  party ball of prizes). Kirby becomes a snowman head at the end and bursts the area open: rift.
Rift: wall comes down from above. Go down breaking icicles.
  SPHERE 3 and 4: Spark Sphere Doomer.

STAGE 4-5 (4 spheres). No mid-boss.
Area 1: outskirts of an ice ruin.
Area 2: path splits; right is gated. Go left past a Wapod into a round-trip door; take the key from
  the Knuckle Joe. Back to the gate.
Area 3: long hall with sliding giant ice blocks. Crackler clears the way.
  SPHERE 1: round-trip door in the floor between two sliding ice blocks. Enter WHILE holding the
  Crackler. Inside, blow away the metal blocks.
  SPHERE 2: at the end of the hall get Fire (Galbo) or keep Fighter. In the next room melt the ice
  blocks to reveal a round-trip door to a bomb block puzzle.
Area 4: Shelts, a ladder up.
Area 5: split, one gated. Go right for the key (Hunter Scarfies get angry). Bring it to the gate.
Area 6, dark hall: carry the candle. Tricky jumps, Point Stars mark the way.
  SPHERE 3: ice barrier in the middle of the hall; melt it with the candle or Fire. Round-trip door;
  inside use Ice.
Area 7: carry a key past Hunter Scarfies to the exit gate.
  SPHERE 4: carry a key past the Scarfies and open the gate on the LEFT.

STAGE 4-6 BOSS: GORIATH. Prize: the emblem.
Phase 1: throws snowballs and ice from the walls (inhale for Ice), long spinning punches, hip
  drops from the wall and from the ground. Keep moving; stars appear where his attacks land.
Phase 2 (golden hair): three energy blasts at angles, a rocket hip drop that bounces between walls
  then homes in after five bounces, falling icicles after a ground pound (inhale for Ice).
  The floor breaks and drops everyone lower up to four times.

LEVEL 5: NUTTY NOON (20 spheres)
=====================================================================

STAGE 5-1 (4 spheres). No mid-boss.
Area 1: Parasol essence over the hill, Warp Star at the top.
Area 2: vines and floating ground over a pit. Door in a cloud nook to the right.
Area 3: weak updraft, glide up with Parasol (also blocks falling Foleys).
  SPHERE 1: round-trip door wrapped in Star Blocks. Inside, past Comos on a crumbling bridge.
Area 4: hanging vine platforms under a huge cloud. Cutting abilities can cut them down.
  SPHERE 2: cut an upper platform to reveal a round-trip door. Sphere on a crumbling platform inside.
Area 5: long tornado tunnel; Tornado ability helps.
  SPHERE 3: side tunnel near the end.
Area 6: spiky boulders fall.
  SPHERE 4: stay ahead of the big boulder rolling behind the small one. The sphere is at the end of
  that path, which closes after.

STAGE 5-2 (4 spheres). Super Ability: Flare Beam. Mid-bosses: Moundo, Spark Sphere Doomer.
Area 1: castle bridge in the clouds.
Area 2: columns moving up and down, many flyers (Bomb is good).
  SPHERE 1: carry a Balloon Bomb across the moving columns and throw it at the big metal barricade.
  Side door behind it: bomb challenge.
Area 3: tower of rooms with pull switches; only one switch per room opens the way up.
Area 4: bridge and Moundo (gives Stone).
  SPHERE 2: in the next hall, pound the row of stakes with Stone, but do NOT pound the last one.
Area 5: Flare Beam lights the Beam Lamps in narrow halls. Light them all for the rift.
Rift: shoot star bullets at bomb blocks behind moving barriers.
  SPHERE 3 and 4: Spark Sphere Doomer. Cannons to the goal.

STAGE 5-3 (4 spheres). No mid-boss.
Area 1: climb ropes in the tower base. Beam opens a 1-Up cache.
Area 2: outside wall, wind and Shotzos. A Crackler helps.
Area 3: race a Key Dee down the inside.
  SPHERE 1: beat the Key Dee to the bottom.
Area 4: other side, ladders, Volttzos, Paclouds.
  SPHERE 2: break the bomb block in the wall; round-trip door; follow the path past Paclouds.
Area 5: Hi-Jump inside.
  SPHERE 3: switch and gate challenge; hit it and hurry.
Area 6: rooms with pull switches; Armor Waddle Dees fall on you.
Area 7: top of the tower, cannon to the last area.
  SPHERE 4: high up, partly hidden by a cloud, near the Owgulf. Goal just right of the Owgulf.

STAGE 5-4 (4 spheres). Super Ability: Grand Hammer. Mid-bosses: Super Bonkers, Spark Sphere Doomer.
Area 1: Stomper Boot over open clouds. One misstep and you fall.
  SPHERE 1: below the main path near the end is a floor of Gordos. Stomp the gold block above it
  with the boot, walk over the Gordos, sphere on the far side.
Area 2: cloud castle steps; ropes, Gigatzos on the walls.
  SPHERE 2: swinging on a rope, between two Gigatzos firing at each other.
Area 3, inside the tower: Super Bonkers gives Grand Hammer. Hit stakes to open the path to a giant
  bell. You are launched up to hit the bell; it breaks and opens the rift.
Rift: interlocking moving platforms.
  SPHERE 3 and 4: Spark Sphere Doomer. Up the tower to the goal.

STAGE 5-5 (4 spheres). Tower of Mid-Bosses. Has a secret path.
You only need ONE of the two paths. Spheres count for both.
Main path (enter the door):
  Gigant Edge, then cut the rope in the next room: SPHERE 1.
  Moundo, then break metal blocks: SPHERE 2.
  Water Galboros, then douse fire blocks with Water: SPHERE 3.
  Dubior, then use Spark in the next room: SPHERE 4.
  Exit right; Maxim Tomato at the end.
Secret path (harder): float above the entrance door and go through the barred window.
  Water Galboros (Water breaks fire blocks): SPHERE 1. King Doo. Dubior (Spark): SPHERE 2.
  Kibble Blade and Gigant Edge (cut rope): SPHERE 3. Bonkers and Moundo (pound stake): SPHERE 4.
  Exit left.

STAGE 5-6 BOSS: GRAND DOOMER. Prize: the mast. Ultra Sword stage.
Phase 1: glide dives (duck most, but not the low dive), energy bullets (inhale and spit back),
  a big energy bomb (Super Inhale it), dark holes in the background, and a rift that opens under
  you (shake to escape).
Phase 2 (half): faster, rams from the background, spiral shots, more rifts.
Phase 3: a shield; only Super Abilities hurt it. Enemies he calls drop Super Abilities. Four
  super hits break the shield (he can rebuild it if left alone). Then finish him.

=====================================================================
LEVEL 6: EGG ENGINES (21 spheres)
=====================================================================

STAGE 6-1 (3 spheres). Super Ability: Monster Flame. Mid-boss: Ice Sphere Doomer.
Area 1: walkway with Volttzos firing. A fuse cannon leads to an optional area.
Area 2: crumbling bridges. Sleep essences around; avoid them.
Area 3: cannons past deadly clamping machines; time each shot.
  SPHERE 1: on purpose, slam into one of the clampers; it leads to a round-trip door with a similar
  cannon challenge.
Area 4: Volttzo gauntlet; Spark helps.
Area 5: Invincible Candy hall. A round-trip door needs a cutting ability.
Area 6, factory: Monster Flame. Burn all bomb machines; light the fuse of the giant one at the top
  for the rift.
Rift: conveyors going left, use enemies to break bomb blocks, dodge Gordos.
  SPHERE 2 and 3: Ice Sphere Doomer. Goal just left.

STAGE 6-2 (4 spheres). Mid-boss: Dubior.
Halls of spiked boxes, Star Block walls, Degouts and spike bars, then rooms with falling drills.
  SPHERE 1: get an ability that douses fire (Ice or Water from the drill-room enemies). In the next
  area a piston pushes up into fire blocks in the ceiling; the sphere is above them.
Rocky and Volttzo area: you need Stone.
  SPHERE 2: swim the channel, then in an air pocket use Stone to break the metal blocks.
Then underwater saws, a switchback hall, hazards, Dubior (gives Spark), and an elevator.
  SPHERE 3: on the elevator, use Spark on the row of bomb blocks in the ceiling; the third one opens
  the sphere.
Final area: pistons crash together, Hunter Degouts chase.

=====================================================================
LEVEL 8: ANOTHER DIMENSION (ending)
=====================================================================

After Landia, Magolor reveals he wanted the Master Crown.

Part 1, shooter: you ride Landia. Keep firing, enemy shots can be shot down. Avoid crushing cave
  walls. Leapers split into missiles when destroyed; move after killing them. In the second part,
  dodge the crystals you cannot break, shoot wall switches to open gates. The spin move goes
  through crystals but use it as a last resort.

Part 2, Lor Starcutter: Magolor attacks with the ship's own parts: emblem shots, wing boomerangs,
  homing oars, a mast vortex, shields, charging. At half: big colored energy balls, fires from
  the background, a whirlwind. Keep shooting.

Part 3, Magolor: you keep your ability; essences for Spear, Spark, Fighter, Water, Whip, Sword.
  He throws dark orbs (can be knocked back), floor spikes, jumps into the background to fire,
  calls Para-Doomers. At half: a vortex pulling you in (run hard the other way) and a beam
  covering the floor.
  Then he puts up a star shield and summons enemies that give Super Abilities. Hit him with a
  super attack while he is in the foreground. Every two hits he takes your ability. The last
  one is Ultra Sword. When the shield breaks, shake (or tap directions fast) to push your last
  strike through.

Part 4, Magolor second form (Master Crown): juggles enemies and throws them; the first one gives
  back your ability. Spikes, dark orbs, travels through rifts, a beam, then at half his own
  versions of the Super Abilities and beams through rifts. Beat him and the crown shatters.
  Ending and credits.
